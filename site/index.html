01 / The correction desk

Current truth is not the whole record.

Calendars are operational data. Meetings move, providers go offline, and yesterday’s useful plan quietly disappears beneath today’s sync.

Snapshotter keeps encrypted local editions and compares each against the last. A cancelled appointment is shown with its former time; a moved one keeps the earlier form ready for export.
