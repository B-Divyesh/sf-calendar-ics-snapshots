Privacy

Your calendar stays in a local vault.

Calendar Snapshotter keeps calendar copies and optional CalDAV details in an encrypted local vault. The app cannot reset a lost vault passphrase.

Network requests

The app contacts a calendar URL only when you choose to import from it. It contacts the license service only after you enter a license token.

The website has no analytics, advertising, remote fonts, or tracking scripts. Calendar content is not sent to the website.

Purchases

Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record for the optional one-time license. Checkout and refund records are handled under its own privacy terms.

Deletion and contact

Removing the application’s local data removes its vault. Restore files remain wherever you exported them. Send privacy questions to privacy@sociobot.in.
