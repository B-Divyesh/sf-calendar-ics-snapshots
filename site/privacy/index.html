Privacy

Your calendar stays on your device.

Calendar Snapshotter stores snapshots and CalDAV credentials in an AES-256-GCM encrypted local vault. Your passphrase is used locally to derive the encryption key and is never transmitted or stored.

Network requests

The app contacts only calendar URLs you configure and Sociobot’s license verification service if you enter a paid license. Calendar content is never sent to Sociobot. The website uses no analytics, cookies, advertising, remote fonts, or tracking scripts.

Purchase records

Sociobot/Dodo acts as merchant of record for license purchases and processes the information required for payment, receipts, fraud prevention, and refunds under its own legal obligations. The app stores your license token locally.

Deletion and contact

Removing the application’s local data deletes its encrypted vault. Restore files you exported remain wherever you saved them. Privacy questions may be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
